## v3.4.0 -- April 4, 2026

**Mathematical Integrity Architecture, Data Readiness Gate, Formula Audit**

- **1,874 Automated Tests with CI Gate** -- Every deployment to production must pass the full test suite (1,874 tests across 43 files) before Vercel allows the build to complete. Zero chance of deploying broken math.
- **Formula Correctness Audit** -- Audited 45+ formulas against industry standards. Fixed 5 material errors: win rate denominator (bowtie), LTV/CAC missing gross margin (scoring), CAC payback missing gross margin (dashboard), NRR conflation with total growth (decision-engine), Rule of 40 linear vs compounding annualization (org-metrics). Resolved 3 cross-file inconsistencies in burn multiple, magic number, and forecast commit weights.
- **Data Readiness Gate** -- New enforcement layer that checks CRM data quality before scoring runs. Three states: ready (85%+), degraded (60-84%), blocked (<60% or critical issues). Banner displays on all authenticated pages when data quality is insufficient.
- **Record-Level Data Quality Drill-Down** -- New `/api/data-readiness/affected-records` endpoint identifies every specific account with data issues, what's wrong, and severity. No more "150 accounts have problems" without knowing which 150.
- **Stale Contact Signal Cascade** -- Detects contacts with no engagement for 4+ months (120 days). Generates cascading signals: stale contact data, no active champion, no active executive buyer, no active stakeholder (CRITICAL). A contact that exists but hasn't engaged is functionally no contact.
- **Scoring-to-Source Consistency Tests** -- New test class that verifies scoring audit decomposition matches actual contact, renewal, and activity data. Catches the class of bug where displayed scores don't reflect reality.
- **Seed Data Integrity Fix** -- `seed-scores.ts` now queries actual contacts, renewals, and activities to derive scoring inputs instead of generating random values. Account 360 score decomposition now reflects real data.
- **Cross-Endpoint Consistency: Full Coverage** -- Expanded from 11 to 32 cross-endpoint tests covering all endpoint pairs that display overlapping metrics (expansion, territory economics, decision engine, TAM/SAM, budget variance, NRR impact, leads, plays, all scoring models).
- **Module Tier Gating** -- 18 pages enforce module-based access control. Shows "Module Not Activated" UI instead of silent redirect when a module isn't unlocked.
- **MCP Server: 22 Tools** -- Added `get_data_readiness` and `get_affected_records` tools for AI-assisted data quality monitoring.

## v3.3.0 -- April 4, 2026

**Renewal Forecasting, Procurement Intelligence, Product Affinity, Cohort Intelligence, Playbook Evolution, Board Narratives**

- **Renewal Forecasting Engine** -- Per-renewal probability model with risk factor and positive signal decomposition. Computes org-level GRR/NRR projections weighted by ARR. Inputs include health score, risk score, engagement, disposition, days to renewal, active playbooks, critical signals, NPS, contract tenure, and deal size.
- **Procurement Intelligence** -- Maps each account's fiscal year into a complete procurement calendar with budget planning, approval, and allocation windows. Assesses renewal-to-budget alignment, generates milestone-based engagement timelines, forecasts budget cycle status per quarter, and identifies procurement risks including misalignment, late engagement, budget freeze, election cycle, and complex procurement.
- **Product Affinity Matrix** -- Computes co-adoption rates, cross-sell timelines, and ARR uplift for every product pair, both overall and per segment. Generates prioritized expansion signals scored by multiple weighted components. Four-state signal lifecycle (pending/pitched/won/dismissed). Extends the Expansion Pipeline with product-pair intelligence.
- **Cohort Intelligence** -- Auto-generates cohorts by vintage quarter, segment, territory, and rep. Computes per-cohort NRR, GRR, churn, expansion, and contraction. Generates 36-month vintage retention curves. Detects anomalies including low NRR, accelerating churn, and health score decline.
- **Playbook Evolution** -- Closed-loop play effectiveness tracking. Groups completed plays by template, segment, and tier, then computes win rates, average time to completion, ARR impact, and health delta. Generates play recommendations using segment affinity scoring and signal severity. Flags underperforming templates.
- **Board Narrative Generation** -- AI-powered board-ready narratives using Claude. Five narrative types (executive summary, QBR, renewal forecast, territory health, pipeline update) with three tone options (board formal, exec concise, detailed analytical). Pre-computed KPI injection ensures generated numbers match verified PILLAR data. Full data snapshot saved for auditability.

## v3.2.0 -- March 31, 2026

**Revenue Impact, Expansion Pipeline, Governance Enforcement**

- **Revenue Impact Dashboard** -- New `/impact` page with signal-to-outcome attribution. Traces every dollar PILLAR touched: signal generated, play triggered, outcome logged. KPIs: ARR Protected, Save Rate, Avg Days Early, Pipeline Influenced. Expandable attribution chain rows with account detail, signal context, and play task completion status.
- **Expansion Pipeline Dashboard** -- New `/expansion` page with product heatmap (accounts x products), whitespace ARR calculation, expansion propensity scoring, recommended next product, and active expansion signals.
- **Mandatory Play Outcomes** -- When all play tasks are completed, an outcome modal requires an outcome (Renewed, Churned, Expanded, Lost) before the play can close. Auto-updates linked renewal disposition and logs governance entry.
- **SLA Enforcement** -- Signal acknowledgment SLAs and play SLA breach detection. Generates escalation signals and governance log entries for non-compliance.
- **Starbridge API Key Self-Service** -- Customers can now enter their Starbridge API key directly in Settings > Integrations. Key encrypted and stored per-org.
- **`complete_play` MCP Tool** -- New MCP tool for AI agents to close plays with mandatory outcomes via Claude Desktop, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible client.
