# Expansion Signals

Expansion signals identify growth opportunities on existing accounts. These are positive signals indicating readiness to buy more.

## Signal Types

| Signal Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Expansion Ready | Expansion propensity score exceeds threshold |
| Whitespace Opportunity | Significant product whitespace detected |
| Usage Threshold Met | Product usage exceeded adoption threshold |
| Cross-sell Signal | Cross-sell conditions met (high adoption + whitespace) |
| Strategic Alignment | Starbridge: institution strategic themes match product capabilities |
| Grant Funding Available | Starbridge: grant funding available for relevant categories |
| Low Adoption Risk | Product adoption dangerously low (risk of removal) |

## Recommended Actions

| Signal | Typical Action |
|--------|---------------|
| Expansion ready | Schedule expansion discovery call, prepare whitespace analysis |
| Whitespace opportunity | Share product roadmap, propose pilot for new modules |
| Cross-sell signal | Propose complementary product, reference similar customer |
| Strategic alignment | Align proposal to strategic plan language, use institution themes in pitch |
| Grant funding available | Prepare grant-aligned proposal, connect with procurement on timeline |
| Low adoption risk | Schedule training/enablement session, assign CSM for adoption review |

All expansion signal thresholds are configurable per organization.

> Detailed threshold configurations and severity logic are available in the PILLAR Implementation Guide provided to active customers.
