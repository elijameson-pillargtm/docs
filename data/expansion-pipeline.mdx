# Expansion Pipeline

The Expansion Pipeline dashboard shows the scored expansion opportunity across your entire account base — which accounts have product whitespace, how ready they are to expand, and what the dollar opportunity is.

## The Finite TAM Argument

In a finite market (~13,000 school districts, ~2,000 higher ed institutions), the fastest path to ARR growth isn't net new logos — it's expanding inside accounts you already serve. PILLAR maps product whitespace and scores expansion propensity across the base.

## Product Heatmap

The core view is a grid of **accounts (rows) x products (columns)**:

| Cell State | Color | Meaning |
|------------|-------|---------|
| Owned, high adoption (60%+) | Green | Healthy product relationship |
| Owned, low adoption (<60%) | Amber | Adoption risk — may not renew this product |
| Not owned | Grey | Whitespace opportunity — estimated ARR shown |

## KPI Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Whitespace ARR** | Total estimated ARR across all missing products for all accounts |
| **High Propensity** | Count of accounts with expansion propensity >= 60 |
| **Avg Adoption** | Average product adoption percentage across owned products |
| **Expansion Signals** | Active signals in the EXPANSION and STARBRIDGE families |

## Expansion Propensity Scoring

Each account receives an expansion propensity score (0-100) based on:

- **Health score** — healthy accounts are more likely to expand
- **Whitespace percentage** — more missing products = more opportunity
- **Champion engagement** — is there an active champion who can advocate?
- **Adoption rate** — high adoption on existing products signals readiness
- **Budget context** — is the district in growth, stable, or cutting?

| Score Range | Label | Meaning |
|-------------|-------|---------|
| 70-100 | High | Account is ready for expansion conversation |
| 40-69 | Medium | Potential exists but needs nurturing |
| 0-39 | Low | Focus on retention before expansion |

## Recommended Next Product

For each account, PILLAR recommends the highest-value missing product based on:
- Annual price of the missing product
- Fit with the account's existing product mix
- District characteristics and priorities

## Expandable Account Rows

Click any row to see:
- **Account summary** — ARR, health, whitespace %, adoption %, link to Account 360
- **Missing products** — list with estimated ARR per product
- **Recommended next product** — highlighted with blue badge
- **Expansion signals** — active signals from EXPANSION and STARBRIDGE families

## API Endpoint

```
GET /api/expansion
```

Returns `kpi`, `products` (full product catalog), and `pipeline` (all accounts with product detail, whitespace, propensity, signals).

## Access

Available to: CRO/CEO, VP Sales, VP CS, RevOps

Navigate to: `/expansion` or click "Expansion" in the Command Center nav pills.
