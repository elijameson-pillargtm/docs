# Starbridge Conference Intelligence

Starbridge tracks education and government technology conferences, providing vendor presence, attendee personas, and engagement opportunities.

## Conference Data

Each conference record includes:

- Conference name, description, and website
- Dates and location (city, state)
- Expected attendee count and attendee personas
- Estimated participation cost range
- Vendor count and known vendor attendees
- Industry verticals represented
- AI-generated vendor landscape summary

## Signal Generation

Conference data generates informational signals when a relevant conference is detected for accounts in your pipeline.

## Use Cases

- Identify conferences where target accounts will be present
- Discover competitor vendor presence at events
- Plan conference marketing strategy based on attendee personas
- Track ROI of conference participation by linking attendee meetings to pipeline
