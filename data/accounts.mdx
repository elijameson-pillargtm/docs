# Accounts

Accounts are the core entity in PILLAR, representing organizations (school districts, companies, institutions) that are customers or prospects. Each account record combines CRM data, PILLAR scoring outputs, and Starbridge district intelligence.

## Core Information

Each account tracks:

- **Identity** -- Name, domain, industry, location
- **Classification** -- Segment (Enterprise, Mid-Market, SMB), tier, district type
- **Ownership** -- Account owner assignment and territory
- **Revenue** -- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- **Status** -- Active, churned, or prospect

## Scoring Outputs

PILLAR continuously computes and updates scores for each account:

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Health Score | Composite account health (0-100) from 8 category scores |
| Priority Score | Account prioritization based on revenue, risk, expansion potential, and market context |
| Risk Score | Churn risk level from multi-variable renewal risk model |
| Expansion Propensity | Likelihood of expansion based on multiple indicators |
| Whitespace | Product catalog gap percentage |
| Health Trend | Historical health score data points for trend visualization |

## Starbridge District Intelligence

When enriched with Starbridge data, accounts gain 120+ additional data points across:

- **Identity and Geography** -- Common names, parent organization, address, coordinates
- **Budget and Financial** -- Operating budget, technology budget, budget trends, spending propensity
- **Procurement Intelligence** -- Procurement complexity, cooperative purchasing agreements, procurement navigation
- **Innovation and Adoption** -- AI adoption readiness, startup friendliness, technology spending propensity, strategic themes
- **K-12 Demographics** -- School counts, staff counts, grade ranges, locale classification, socioeconomic indicators, SIS/LMS vendors
- **Higher Education** -- Institution type, enrollment, graduation/retention rates, endowment, system affiliation
- **Government** -- Employee FTE, fiscal year, census identifiers

## CRM Sync

Accounts are synced from your connected CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, or Dynamics 365) and enriched with PILLAR scoring outputs that can be pushed back to CRM custom fields.
