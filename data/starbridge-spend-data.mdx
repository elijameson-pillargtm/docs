# Starbridge Spend Data

Starbridge provides publicly-available contract records and purchase order data for buyer organizations. This data powers competitive intelligence, displacement window signals, and procurement pathway analysis.

## Contracts

Public contract records between buyers and vendors, including:

- Contract title and summary
- Effective and expiration dates
- Total contract value
- Buyer and seller names
- Contract status

## Purchase Orders

Individual purchase order line items from public procurement records, including:

- Line item description
- Quantity, unit price, and total amount
- Effective and renewal dates
- Buyer and seller names

## Competitive Intelligence

PILLAR derives competitive intelligence from Starbridge spend data:

- Competitor vendor name and product category
- Contract value, start date, and expiration date
- Procurement method (RFP, coop, sole source)
- Source documentation
- Displacement eligibility assessment

## Signal Generation

Spend data generates signals when:

- **Competitor contracts are approaching expiration** -- Creating displacement windows with higher urgency as expiration approaches
- **Tracked competitor contracts approach renewal** -- Alerting to competitive opportunities

## Scoring Impact

Competitive intelligence feeds into Intent scoring (competitive displacement windows), Risk scoring (competitive threat assessment), and Opportunity Quality (competitive positioning evaluation).
