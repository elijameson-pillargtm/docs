# Starbridge Document Intelligence

Starbridge indexes publicly-available documents from buyer organizations, including board meeting minutes, strategic plans, budget documents, and policy papers.

## Document Types

| Type | Description | How PILLAR Uses It |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Board Minutes | Board of education meeting minutes | Generates signals when product-relevant topics are discussed |
| Strategic Plan | Multi-year strategic plans | Updates strategic themes on the account, feeds scoring |
| Budget | Annual budget documents | Updates budget fields, informs financial scoring |
| Policy | Policy documents and guidelines | Informs procurement complexity analysis |
| Agenda | Meeting agendas | Early indicator of upcoming discussions |

Each document includes the title, AI-generated summary, posting date, effective date, and link to the original file.

## Signal Generation

- **Board Discussion Relevant** -- Fired when AI analysis detects mentions of relevant product categories or strategic priorities in board minutes
- **Strategic Alignment** -- When strategic plan themes align with your product capabilities

## Scoring Impact

Document intelligence feeds into Fit scoring (strategic theme alignment), Intent scoring (board discussion relevance, strategic theme match), and Account Prioritization (strategic alignment).
