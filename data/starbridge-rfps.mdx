# Starbridge RFP Intelligence

Starbridge monitors public procurement portals for RFPs, bids, and solicitations from buyer organizations. Active RFPs generate high-priority signals.

## RFP Data

Each RFP record includes:

- RFP title and AI-generated summary
- Link to the original RFP document
- Release date and submission deadline
- Bid submission instructions
- Issuing organization name

## Signal Generation

Active RFPs generate critical-severity pipeline signals because RFPs are time-sensitive buying events. Recommended action: Review RFP requirements, assess fit, prepare response.

## Scoring Impact

RFP activity is one of the strongest intent signals in PILLAR's scoring engine. An active RFP significantly increases the Intent category score, and its presence improves forecast confidence through the procurement alignment dimension.
