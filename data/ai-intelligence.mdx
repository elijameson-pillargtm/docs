# AI Intelligence Layer

PILLAR integrates Anthropic's Claude AI to provide contextual intelligence across scoring, signals, account analysis, and recommended actions. All AI features use organization-scoped data isolation.

## AI Capabilities

### 1. Pre-Computed Signal Actions

When CRITICAL signals are generated, Claude analyzes the full account context and produces role-specific recommended actions with reasoning, urgency assessment, and confidence scoring. If the AI service is unavailable, PILLAR falls back to rule-based action recommendations.

### 2. AI Coaching Intelligence

Comprehensive, per-rep coaching reports generated on-demand from the VP Sales or VP CS Coaching tab. Claude analyzes the rep's entire portfolio -- accounts, pipeline, signals, renewals, activities, and KPIs -- to produce actionable coaching guidance.

**Output includes:**
- Executive summary and overall grade
- Data-backed strengths and development areas
- Priority deals and accounts requiring coaching intervention
- Signal response rate analysis
- Pipeline health assessment
- Quota outlook with will-they-hit assessment
- Prioritized 1:1 agenda items

### 3. Account Intelligence

On-demand AI analysis accessible from Account 360:

| Capability | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Account Intelligence | Deep account analysis with deal strategy, risk assessment, expansion opportunities |
| Account Narrative | AI-generated account summary for meeting prep and QBRs |
| Action Plan | Strategic action plan generation for specific accounts |
| Rep Coaching | Comprehensive coaching report for the account owner |
| Board Narrative | Board-ready narrative for executive reporting |

### 4. Role-Specific Action Routing

Each signal type has role-appropriate recommended actions:

| Role | Action Style |
|------|-------------|
| CRO/CEO | Strategic, executive-level intervention |
| VP Sales | Coaching, pipeline-focused guidance |
| VP CS | Retention, relationship-focused recommendations |
| RevOps | Analytical, data-focused insights |
| Rep/CSM | Tactical, action-oriented tasks |

### 5. Weekly Digest Intelligence

The weekly digest email includes AI-curated highlights:
- Top risk accounts with context
- Pipeline movement summary
- Recommended focus areas for the week

## Data Privacy

- AI prompts include only the authenticated organization's data
- No cross-org data leakage in AI context
- AI outputs are stored per-signal, scoped by organization
- Anthropic does not train on API data (per their data policy)
