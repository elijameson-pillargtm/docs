# ICP Fit Scoring

The Fit category evaluates how well an account or lead matches your Ideal Customer Profile. It analyzes firmographic characteristics, demographic data, and stakeholder personas to produce a fit score.

## What It Measures

ICP Fit scoring evaluates multiple dimensions:

- **Revenue/ARR alignment** -- Does the account's size match your sweet spot?
- **Segment fit** -- Enterprise, Mid-Market, or SMB alignment
- **Organization size** -- Student count, employee count, or other size metrics
- **Technology spending propensity** -- Likelihood of investing in technology (Starbridge-enhanced)
- **Innovation readiness** -- AI adoption readiness and openness to new vendors (Starbridge-enhanced)
- **Budget capacity** -- Technology budget size relative to your pricing
- **Strategic alignment** -- Whether the institution's priorities match your product capabilities
- **Procurement fit** -- Procurement complexity and cooperative purchasing availability
- **Account tier** -- Overall tier classification based on potential
- **Decision-maker presence** -- Whether your primary contacts match ideal buyer personas

## Configurable ICP Profiles

PILLAR supports fully configurable ICP profiles per organization:

### Company Fit Tiers
Define organization size tiers based on your market. Default uses enrollment-based tiers for K-12, but can be customized to revenue, headcount, or any other metric.

### Segment Weights
Configure the relative value of each market segment (Enterprise, Mid-Market, SMB) to your business.

### Persona Fit Rules
Map job titles to buyer personas and score contacts based on how well they match your ideal buying committee. PILLAR supports configurable title-to-persona mappings with tiered scoring.

### ICP Title Tiers
Organize titles into tiers (e.g., C-suite, Director-level, Manager-level, Individual Contributor) with configurable scoring impact.

## Fit Classification

| Classification | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| Strong Fit | Account closely matches your ICP across multiple dimensions |
| Moderate Fit | Account matches some ICP criteria but has gaps |
| Weak Fit | Account has limited alignment with your ICP |

Fit classification thresholds are configurable per organization.

> Detailed scoring methodology, including exact tier definitions, persona mappings, and weight distributions, is available in the PILLAR Implementation Guide provided to active customers.
