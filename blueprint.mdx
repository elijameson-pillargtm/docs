## What It Measures

The assessment evaluates your organization across the 5 pillars of GTM operational excellence:

| Pillar | Categories | Questions | What It Evaluates |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **I. Strategy** | 5 | 24 | ICP & market definition, territory design & coverage, revenue model & planning, pricing & packaging, public sector & district readiness |
| **II. People** | 5 | 25 | Organizational design & roles, quota modeling & capacity, hiring & onboarding, enablement & training, culture & motivation |
| **III. Process** | 7 | 39 | Pipeline creation & qualification, deal progression & closing, onboarding & adoption, renewal & retention, expansion motion, operating cadences, cross-functional handoffs |
| **IV. Systems** | 5 | 28 | CRM architecture & hygiene, tech stack & connector health, data governance & source of truth, signal infrastructure, AI & automation readiness |
| **V. Metrics** | 5 | 26 | Pipeline metrics & forecasting, revenue attribution, retention & expansion metrics, executive & board reporting, metric governance |

**Total: 27 categories, 142 questions**

## How Scoring Works

Each question is scored on a 3-point scale:

| Response | Score | Meaning |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Doesn't Exist | 0 | This capability does not exist in your organization |
| Partially There | 1 | Some elements exist but are inconsistent or incomplete |
| Fully Deployed | 2 | This capability is well-established and consistently executed |

**Pillar scores** are calculated as the percentage of maximum possible points within that pillar. For example, if Strategy has 24 questions (max 48 points) and you score 36, your Strategy pillar score is 75%.

**Overall score** is the percentage of total points scored across all 142 questions (max 284 points).

## Maturity Tiers

Your overall score maps to one of four maturity tiers:

| Tier | Score Range | Description |
|------|------------|-------------|
| **Foundation** | 0–39% | Core GTM processes are missing or ad hoc. Significant structural gaps exist across most pillars. Immediate foundational work is needed. |
| **Emerging** | 40–64% | Basic GTM structure exists but execution is inconsistent. Key processes are partially defined. Focus areas are becoming clear. |
| **Mature** | 65–85% | GTM operations are established and repeatable. Most processes are documented and governed. Optimization and intelligence layering are the next step. |
| **Advanced** | 86–100% | GTM operations are data-driven and continuously improving. Processes are automated, signals are routed, and the organization operates as a revenue machine. |

## Your Report

After completing the assessment, you receive a comprehensive report including:

1. **Overall maturity tier** with composite score
2. **Per-pillar scores** with visual breakdowns
3. **About your company** — AI-researched company intelligence (funding, scale, market segment, competitive landscape)
4. **Your market context** — ESSER/grant exposure, competitive pressures, and why your specific gaps matter right now
5. **Strengths analysis** — what your organization does well, with specific category-level detail
6. **Gap analysis** — where the biggest opportunities for improvement exist
7. **Next steps** — concrete actions to take in each pillar, with recommended PILLAR modules
8. **Key risk** — the single biggest risk your gaps create, with timeline and impact
9. **90-day maturity roadmap** — a phased improvement plan (Weeks 1–4, 5–8, 9–12)

Reports are generated using AI analysis enriched with web-researched company intelligence, calibrated against the actual scoring data. Every insight is grounded in your specific responses and your company's real market position.
